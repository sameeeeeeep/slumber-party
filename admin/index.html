sponsors & deliverables

—

every deal has two sides, so each sponsor has two lists: what we get from them, and what we owe back. Cash deals carry an amount; barter deals carry the goods. Everything we owe has an owner and a date, because "we'll post something" is how a sponsor gets missed.

never have I ever

—

one question at a time. Guests open /never and get two buttons; every answer lands on /never/wall as a name bubble on the side they picked, and when someone changes their mind the bubble crosses the middle. Write the questions here in the order you want to ask them, then ask this to put one on the wall — only one is live at a time. An answer can be changed, so a misfire costs one tap.

the puzzle

—

an image cut into pieces, handed out one tap at a time. Guests open /puzzle and tap; the picture assembles on /puzzle/wall with their names on the pieces as they land. Nobody's phone ever holds the image, so it can't be seen early. Only one puzzle is live at a time — that's the one the wall draws.

the nail board

—

the 20-minute round: guests add nail designs at /board and the wall at /board/wall fills up as they land. Everything added is kept — nothing here expires or tidies itself away. save everything pulls the whole round down as a zip: every picture, named for its style and whose it is, plus the csv and the ranked tally. Hide anything that shouldn't be on a projector; it drops off the wall within three seconds. clear the board is only for when you want the bucket empty — take the zip first.
