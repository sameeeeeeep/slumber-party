sponsors & deliverables

—

every deal has two sides, so each sponsor has two lists: what we get from them, and what we owe back. Cash deals carry an amount; barter deals carry the goods. Everything we owe has an owner and a date, because "we'll post something" is how a sponsor gets missed.

BFFs

—

brands giving us product — hampers, gifting, things we keep. No money changes hands and nothing is owed back, which is what makes them different from a sponsor. Whatever they send goes into a hamper below.
